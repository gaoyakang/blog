I often fall into a profound sense of emptiness, believing life has no meaning, like being in a dark space with no boundaries, no direction. What guides me out of this emptiness is a comprehensive set of basic thinking principles I've developed. It's like the <mark>origin coordinates</mark> in the darkness, allowing me to quickly locate myself and escape existential anxiety. I've internalized these into a series of questions.

---

## 1. What Am I?

I am the combination of **Physical Me**, **Mental Me**, and **Social Me**.

- **Physical Me**: An objectively existing, tangible entity
    - **Survival**: Food, clothing, shelter, transportation
    - **Health**: Sleep, diet, digestion, exercise, psychology
    - **Fitness**: Muscle, skincare, fashion

- **Mental Me**: The distillation of all experiences from birth to present
    - **Id**: Primitive desires
    - **Ego**: Oscillates between id and superego
    - **Superego**: The ideal, perfect version of myself

- **Social Me**: The version of me that exists in others' minds

---

## 2. Why Does Mental Me Drive Physical Me to Act?

- To **survive**
- For **emotions**: guilt, dedication
- To **realize self-worth**
